Why site owners use it

Visitors stay on your site

A tool page answers what people search for ("compress pdf", "jadwal sholat", "qr code generator") and keeps them on your domain instead of sending them to another site.

No server load, no API bills

Processing happens on the visitor's device with WebAssembly. No upload limits to raise, no quota, no third-party API key.

Private by design

Files never leave the device. Nothing is sent to toowl or anyone else, which is easy to explain in your own privacy policy.

Won't fight your theme

Each tool is a web component in a Shadow DOM: your theme's CSS can't break it. Light, dark or follow the visitor; English or Indonesian.

Questions

What do I need?
WordPress 6.5 or newer and PHP 7.4 or newer. Works with block themes and classic themes; the shortcode works in page builders.
How do I install Pro?
Download the ZIP from your Gumroad library, then Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin. Paste your key in Settings → toowl. If the free plugin is active, Pro switches it off and keeps its blocks and settings.
How do updates work?
wp-admin shows when a new version is out. Download the new ZIP from your Gumroad library and upload it with "Replace current with uploaded".
Does it send my visitors' files anywhere?
No. Every tool runs from the plugin's own folder in the visitor's browser. The only outside calls are from your server: the license check with Gumroad and the update check. See the privacy policy.
Refunds?
If a tool doesn't work on your site, write within 14 days and you get your money back.
